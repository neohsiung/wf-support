Watch Face Support

English · 繁體中文

Contact

Questions, bug reports or refund help: support@litov.app. Please include your watch model and the name of the watch face.

Getting started

  1. Install the watch face from Google Play on the watch, or on the phone paired with it.
  2. On the watch, touch and hold the current watch face, swipe to the new one and tap it.
  3. To change colours or the four text slots, touch and hold the face and tap Edit.

Requirements

Wear OS 5.1 or later (Watch Face Format). Slots show data from apps on your watch; any slot can be left empty.

Privacy policy

These watch faces do not collect, store, share or transmit personal data. They contain no code, no ads and no analytics, and request no special permissions. Information shown in a slot (for example steps, battery or date) comes from the other apps you choose and stays on your watch. Google Play and your watch maker process purchases and installs under their own policies.

support.litov.app does not use cookies or analytics tools. The one exception is the waitlist form on the Day And Hour page. Tally runs that form and loads it only on that page. We use the email and watch model you enter only to send the one launch email and to count which watches people wear. We do not share them. To be removed, write to support@litov.app.

litov.app uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-TY6DFCRQMP) only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. Before that, nothing from Google loads and no analytics cookie is set. If you decline, or close the banner without choosing, nothing is loaded either.

  • Purpose: aggregate traffic measurement: visits, sources and which pages help. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Cookies set after you accept: _ga and _ga_TY6DFCRQMP. Expiry: 13 months.
  • What is measured: page views, plus three events: a click on a product card, a submit of the email signup form, and a submit of the waitlist form. Google receives these together with technical data such as your IP address and browser.
  • Retention: 14 months.
  • Google signals: not enabled.
  • Withdraw consent: click “Cookie settings” in the footer of any litov.app page and choose Decline. The _ga cookies are deleted and measurement stops. Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage), so clearing site data asks you again.
  • Processor: Google acts as our processor for this measurement. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Last updated: 2026-11-05.

安裝：從 Google Play 在手錶（或配對的手機）安裝；在手錶上長按目前錶面，滑到新錶面並點選。要換顏色或四個文字欄位，長按錶面後點「編輯」。

需求：Wear OS 5.1 以上。欄位顯示手錶上其他 app 提供的資料，任何欄位都可以留空。

隱私權政策：這些錶面不蒐集、儲存、分享或傳送任何個人資料。沒有程式碼、廣告或分析工具，也不要求特殊權限。欄位內顯示的資料（例如步數、電量、日期）來自你自己選的其他 app，只留在手錶上。購買與安裝由 Google Play 與手錶廠商依其政策處理。政策若有變更會公告在本頁。最後更新：2026-11-05。

support.litov.app：本站沒有 cookie，也沒有分析工具。唯一的例外是 Day And Hour 頁面上的等待名單表單，由 Tally 提供，只在那一頁載入。你填的 email 與手錶型號只用來寄那一封上架通知，以及統計大家戴哪款手錶，不會分享出去。要刪除請寫信到 support@litov.app。

litov.app：只有在你於 cookie 橫幅按「接受」之後，才會使用 Google Analytics 4（衡量 ID G-TY6DFCRQMP）。在那之前，不會載入任何 Google 資源，也不會設定分析用 cookie。按「拒絕」或沒有選擇就關閉橫幅，同樣不會載入。

  • 目的：彙總的流量統計：造訪次數、來源，以及哪些頁面有幫助。不用來識別你個人。
  • 接受後設定的 cookie：_ga 與 _ga_TY6DFCRQMP。期限：13 個月。
  • 量測內容：頁面瀏覽，加上三個事件：點產品卡片、送出 email 訂閱表單、送出等待名單表單。Google 會連同 IP 位址、瀏覽器等技術資料一併收到。
  • 保留期限：14 個月。
  • Google signals：未啟用。
  • 撤回同意：在 litov.app 任一頁的頁尾點「Cookie 設定」，選「拒絕」。_ga 系列 cookie 會被刪除，統計隨即停止。你的選擇存在瀏覽器的 localStorage，清除網站資料後會再問一次。
  • 受託處理者：這項統計由 Google 擔任我們的受託處理者。請見 Google 隱私權政策與Google 如何使用採用其服務的網站資料。
